THE PROJECTA website concept for a clothing house

A clothing house.
A digital point of view.

A website that invites
a closer look at the clothes.

For MERCER VALE, we developed the digital presentation of a first collection, from reference research to a working interface. Large-scale photography, restrained typography and connected motion establish the direction.

The brief was to preserve the atmosphere of a fashion campaign while giving visitors a clear route to the looks, the house and a conversation.

RESEARCH & STRUCTUREStructure before composition

From reference
to a direction.

We reviewed the structure of ten clothing stores and the writing of five further brands. White Desert informed the composition through image scale, generous pauses and changes in framing.

We adapted those compositional principles to clothing: the collection sets the sequence, and editorial detail gives each photograph a purpose.

DISCOVERYFrom the campaign to a specific piece

A collection
you can browse.

Categories

Women and Men each contain three separate collections. Every tab opens eight photographs of its own: 48 distinct images, with no repeats between collections.

Room for the image

A full-length opening frame anchors an editorial grid of looks and details. The grid adapts to mobile while keeping the Women / Men selection visible.

Specific language

Outerwear, Shirts & tops, Knitwear and Tailoring name what visitors will find. The selected collection has its own heading, eight-image count and direct URL.

INTERFACE DETAILSAn interface that supports exploration

Search, with an answer.

Local search covers all 48 collection photographs and the page sections. A result opens its photograph inside the matching collection. An empty state suggests what to try next.

Room to look closer.

Each full-screen view stays within the selected collection’s eight photographs. Descriptions and the position count follow the frame. Arrow keys navigate; Escape closes the view and restores focus.

MOBILE DIRECTIONA composition for the smaller screen

Designed
for the hand.

On mobile, scale, hierarchy and image behaviour change. The pinned photographic passage becomes a naturally scrolling frame, with touch controls within easy reach.

EDITORIAL COPYWrite about the clothes in the frame

Less promise.
More observation.

Before

Room to be yourself.

A broad promise that could appear on almost any fashion website.

After

The white top.

A sleeveless white top with straight blue denim. Raw hems and dark sandals give the outfit a sharper finish.

We rewrote the English headlines, study descriptions, search, FAQ and contact copy around visible details and the collection’s current stage. Claims about fabrics, factories and durability require evidence before they can be published.

THE DELIVERABLEDesign, copy and a working interface

The first collection.
Before the first order.

The result is a concept website for a clothing house, with its own visual direction, responsive composition and six distinct collections. Each contains eight photographs, with dedicated browsing, search and full-screen viewing.

Checks covered desktop and mobile layouts, all six collection tabs and every eight-image lookbook sequence, alongside search, keyboard access, loading and reduced motion. Sales impact has not been measured; the collection remains in development.
